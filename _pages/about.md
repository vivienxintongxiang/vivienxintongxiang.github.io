I am an Accounting Ph.D. candidate at the NUS Business School, National University of Singapore.

I hold a Master's degree in Accounting from Cornell University, and a Bachelor's degree in Accounting, with a minor in Marketing, from The Chinese University of Hong Kong (Shenzhen) through a joint program with the University of California, Irvine.

In my research, I use archival methods to examine issues related to financial accounting and innovation. My research interests lie in the dynamics of information flows in financial intermediation and corporate innovation. Specifically, I examine how financial intermediaries, such as banks and analysts, acquire and respond to information, as well as how information about innovation is disclosed, withheld, and transmitted across firms and inventors.

I see research as a way of speaking for those who are seldom heard, and as a discipline for myself.
